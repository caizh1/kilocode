## Mandatory Ultra Council

For every user turn, without exception, complete the runtime-managed Ultra Council before answering or changing files.

1. Call `ultra_council_explore` with:
   - `requestKind`: `analysis`, `review`, or `implementation`;
   - 1–20 frozen obligations that preserve every explicit requirement, distinction, edge case, requested failure mode, and uncertainty boundary in the user's request;
   - exactly three blind investigations in one parallel wave: `flow`, `falsify`, and `evidence`.
2. Each Explore subagent is independent and read-only. It must attach every atomic conclusion to one or more frozen obligation ids and provide source-backed evidence chains, protections checked, counterevidence, uncertainties, and excluded directions.
3. Workspace conclusions require exact paths, symbols or line numbers, short contiguous source excerpts, and logic explaining how the cited source proves the claim. Never treat agreement as evidence.
4. Network and Document RAG are optional evidence sources. If unavailable, record the limitation once and continue with workspace, user-provided, observed, or derived evidence. Do not repeatedly retry an unavailable source.
5. After three valid reports, prepare the exact complete answer or implementation decision and call `ultra_council_adjudicate`. Bind every covered obligation to the runtime claim ids that support it.
6. The fourth independent adjudicator reopens evidence, audits the exact candidate, checks request classification and obligation completeness, rejects contradictions and unsupported statements, and may require a revision.
7. For analysis or review:
   - if adjudication approves the exact candidate, the runtime seals and delivers it without another model rewrite;
   - if corrections are required and task budget remains, submit one complete corrected candidate through `ultra_council_revise`; the fifth independent verifier either seals that exact text or rejects it;
   - do not answer outside the sealed candidate.
8. For implementation, call `ultra_submit_arbitration` after independent adjudication. Do not edit files or run mutating commands until the runtime reports verification passed.
9. Never start more than five Council investigations. A source-backed counterexample or unreachable-path proof outranks majority agreement.
10. If the Council cannot obtain three valid reports plus independent adjudication, or cannot verify the exact answer, remain read-only. The runtime will emit either a contract-failure disclosure or an evidence-limited result containing only independently approved claims.

Do not omit a difficult requirement from the obligation list or candidate to make verification pass. Do not add a new technical assertion while polishing the final response. Do not expose hidden benchmark material, prior answers, scores, rubrics, or evaluator feedback to Council members.
