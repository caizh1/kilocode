# 12 Word Export Rules

## 1. Kilo Word route

When the user asks for Word output, generate the `.docx` with Kilo's generic Word tools. Use `create_word_document` for the initial artifact and `apply_word_document_edits` for bounded non-image chapter batches. Do not introduce a task-specific Word pipeline, temporary JSON shell bridge, local Pandoc, local LibreOffice/soffice authoring, `pip install`, `npm install`, `python-docx`, or a standalone Python/Node DOCX script as the primary or fallback route. `08-word-export-input.md`, when used, is an auditable assembly draft, not the final export mechanism or tool payload.

## 2. create_word_document arguments

Before calling `create_word_document`, verify the required Word tools are exposed and complete the target plus every confirmed submodule's fourteen-topic content draft, evidence table, and required diagrams. Freeze the final ordered outline from that completed design-unit census. Build object-shaped top-level arguments with `title`, `language: "zh-CN"`, `documentType: "详细设计"`, `headingNumbering: "decimal"`, optional document fields, and `sections`; never wrap or stringify them. Choose a stable `taskSlug` and `.docx` `outputFile` once.

A full detailed design uses a lightweight initial skeleton containing the real tool-level title, optional document metadata, summary paragraphs, exactly one standalone summary paragraph whose full text is `{{TOC}}`, every final H1/H2/H3 heading in frozen order beginning with Heading 1 `阅读路径`, one unique content anchor for every bounded prose/table batch, all successfully rendered base and focused image blocks, and unique anchors for required figures still marked `MISSING`, but no long prose, large tables, long lists, or code bodies. Do not create the skeleton from only the first few completed submodules. A genuine render failure does not erase its slot or justify deleting its design unit. Do not embed the placeholder in other text, place it inside a chapter, repeat it, or handwrite a directory as Normal paragraphs. Use returned PNG paths instead of base64 whenever possible.

After skeleton creation, fill non-image content in canonical order with strictly serial `apply_word_document_edits` calls. Pass the stable `taskSlug` and `outputFile` explicitly on every mutation so the filename stays stable. One main chapter or confirmed-submodule design unit is the maximum batch size. Split a larger unit across its predeclared H3 anchors, consume every anchor exactly once, and pass every newly returned Word path into the next mutation. Do not use a document-end append when an anchor is absent or ambiguous; repair the skeleton or target a more specific existing H3 anchor first. Structural edits cannot add image blocks; use `insert_mermaid_into_word` at a unique figure heading for a late PNG. Even when `pngPath` exists, pass the exact Mermaid text in the required `source` argument, plus the current `wordPath`, unique `heading`, stable `taskSlug`/`outputFile`, source-mapped `pngPath`, plain Chinese `figureTitle`, complete independent Chinese `altText`, and unique visible caption. If the image skeleton is still too large, create the smallest skeleton that still preserves the full frozen outline, then insert every rendered PNG serially; do not drop headings, units, or coverage slots to reduce the payload.

If a large call fails, reduce it to the lightweight skeleton rather than writing the payload to a temporary file or retrying the same oversized object. Split a failing chapter until the smallest meaningful H3 subsection is isolated. If that subsection still fails, preserve the last successful DOCX, persist the exact continuation point, and disclose the native tool error instead of switching to a third-party generator. Do not replace the failed detailed subsection with a one-line summary. Use assumptions, limitations, gaps, and owner-review items for genuinely incomplete evidence rather than continuing unbounded search.

Sections must preserve the hybrid structure from `10-detail-design-output-templates.md`: chapter 3 contains only context-parent positioning and target handoffs; chapters 4 through 8 together form the target module DesignUnit, with target architecture in chapter 4, its unique business primary in chapter 6, target-internal decomposition with no diagram slot in chapter 7, and target data/code/state views in chapter 8. Chapter 8 references the chapter-6 business figure without reinserting or recounting it. Chapter 9 then contains one continuous chapter per target-owned confirmed submodule in the exact frozen decomposition-table order, followed by cross-module summaries, indexes, coverage, optional differences, and review items. Context parents must not acquire target-style chapters or mandatory five-view images. Target implementation chapter 8 must never appear between child chapters, and a later child batch must never be appended after the closing global chapters. Do not assemble all submodule business flows, code flows, state machines, or objects into detached horizontal chapters.

New detailed-design documents use the Codex `standard_business_brief` baseline: US Letter portrait, one-inch margins, Calibri 11 pt body, the standard blue H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, real numbering, fixed-DXA tables, quiet running header, and right-aligned page number. Put the standalone `{{TOC}}` paragraph after the title/summary and before the first Heading 1 for a full detailed design. After all chapter and late-image mutations, call `materialize_word_fields` with `tocMode: "materialize"`, the stable `taskSlug`, and the stable `outputFile`. The result must be a native Word TOC field with a non-outline directory heading and page separation, not a static list of copied heading text.

## 3. Existing-document update and difference output

For an existing-document update, rebuild conclusions from current source and use `apply_word_document_edits`, `apply_word_template_styles`, `materialize_word_fields`, `normalize_word_table_spec`, `merge_word_documents`, or `diff_word_documents` as appropriate. Preserve unrelated user content and styles where the request is a bounded edit. Do not regenerate unrelated chapters or use the old document as current implementation evidence.

A difference-only request may return the requested diff report without producing a new full Word document. A single-chapter, single-submodule, state-machine-only, or quick update uses the same generic Word tools and only the relevant sections; it must not be expanded into the canonical full-document outline unless the user asks.

## 4. Figures and diagrams

Track separate `CoverageSlot` and `DiagramRequirement` ledgers. `CoverageSlot` contains exactly `5D` base rows. `DiagramRequirement` contains one row per actual base or focused figure using `diagramId -> baseOrFocused -> owningSlot -> complexityInstanceIds -> sourceEvidence -> mmdPath -> sourceStatus -> syntaxValidation -> semanticCoverage -> pngPath -> visibleFigureId -> targetSection -> renderDisposition -> visualQaStatus -> wordInsertionStatus`. Insert every successfully rendered required Mermaid diagram as an image block in the lightweight skeleton at the exact `sections[].blocks[]` position where it is explained:

```json
{
  "type": "image",
  "path": "<pngPath returned by render_mermaid_diagram>",
  "contentType": "image/png",
  "title": "中文图题",
  "caption": "[DU-<designUnitId>/<viewType>/<diagramId>] 中文图注",
  "altText": "完整的中文可访问性说明，源码符号保持原样",
  "width": 480,
  "height": 280
}
```

Use ordered blocks to control surrounding text, image, title, and caption placement. The stable visible figure ID appears exactly once, at the start of the caption; the title is plain Chinese and the alt text is a complete accessible description. Use the render result's cropped display width and height and retain pixel dimensions as QA evidence. Word insertion is inline, centered, aspect-locked, downscale-only, and bounded to 624 CSS px wide and 720 CSS px high. If fitting the page makes labels unreadable, split and rerender the diagram instead of shrinking it again. Keep `.mmd` paths in diagram indexes and evidence ledgers for traceability. If the user explicitly asks for Mermaid source in Word, use a normal code block with Mermaid source; otherwise rendered diagrams must be PNG images.

## 5. Render verification

Before mutation, record the actual baseline relationship-ID set: empty for a new document, or all inspected preserved relationships for an existing document. Inspect immediately after skeleton creation to record `skeletonRelationshipIds` and confirm that the complete frozen target/submodule heading sequence and all content anchors are present before filling any prose. After each late insertion inspect and derive `lateInsertedRelationshipIds` by set difference; after replacement record `replacedRelationshipIds` and verify that the relationship set did not grow; after an intended removal inspect and record only relationships actually absent as `removedRelationshipIds`. Replacing image content normally keeps the relationship ID, and deleting a paragraph or section does not prove its relationship was deleted. After all chapter, late-image, style, and field mutations, make the final `inspect_word_document` call with `maxParagraphs: 1000` and `maxTables: 200`. Require exactly one Title-style paragraph whose text equals the planned title, no remaining `{{TOC}}`, `阅读路径` as the first outline item followed by `术语、范围与证据基线`, and exact equality with the frozen heading sequence. Compare final relationship IDs with the expected set using set difference, not planned-count arithmetic. `imageCount` is only a coarse relationship count; it cannot prove content completeness, drawing placement, or semantic identity.

All mutations of one DOCX must be serialized. Parallel Mermaid rendering is allowed, but parallel calls that edit or insert into the same `sourcePath` are forbidden: each output is a sibling fork and later copies can discard earlier figures. Maintain one authoritative working path and pass the path returned by each successful `apply_word_document_edits`, `insert_mermaid_into_word`, style, or field operation into the next mutation. A disclosed coverage-slot, visible-ID, or unexplained relationship-delta mismatch is not completion; sequentially repair the specific defect and reinspect.

The post-repair inspection must compare the final outline, exact heading order, all fourteen local topics for every design unit, later cross-module chapters, unique caption-only figure-ID paragraphs, table count, and image relationship set with the planned document. It must reject a census that lists more confirmed submodules than the number of complete local chapters, a chapter that contains only a name inventory, and any target or child unit moved outside the frozen order. Call inspection with `maxParagraphs: 1000` and `maxTables: 200`; require `paragraphsTruncated: false` before using paragraph results as a complete visible-ID audit and `tablesTruncated: false` before using table results as complete. Reconcile each actual drawing occurrence to the ledger using `headingPath`, `caption`, visible `[DU-<designUnitId>/<viewType>/<diagramId>]`, `altText`, relationship ID, media path, and content hash. Require zero missing relationships, orphan relationships, missing media targets, duplicate visible IDs, and unexplained duplicate hashes. If either paragraph or table inspection remains truncated, audit the affected evidence on every rendered page; if neither structural nor page evidence is complete, set `acceptanceStatus: PARTIAL`. `imageCount` alone is insufficient.

For the full detailed-design Word workflow, call `render_word_document` after the relationship and outline checks. The tool tries the configured Render Service first and the local LibreOffice/Poppler path second. Record `pageEvidenceStatus` separately from the skill-owned `pageReviewStatus`; the legacy `visualQaStatus: completed` only means page-image evidence exists. A native TOC is complete only when the render result explicitly reports `fieldRefreshStatus: completed`, returns a valid `refreshedDocxPath`, and reports equal nonzero `tocHeadingCount`, `tocEntryCount`, and `tocPageNumberCount`; an updated DOCX without this status, or a local plain conversion, is not proof of field refresh. Make only that verified refreshed DOCX authoritative, then repeat title, outline, TOC, drawing, relationship, and truncation inspection. Open every page PNG at 100%, inspect complex figure/table/code pages again at 200%, fix material risks, rerender the whole document, and repeat the all-page review. Automated page QA, ink ratios, edge checks, or inability to view images cannot be reported as visual pass; if pages cannot actually be opened, set `pageReviewStatus: skipped`. If both render paths are unavailable, continue the Word delivery with `pageEvidenceStatus: unavailable`, disclose the reason, and do not claim page-image visual QA passed. Report DOCX existence separately as `artifactStatus: generated | failed`; report the scoped result as `acceptanceStatus: PASS | PARTIAL`. A generated DOCX with failed TOC refresh or skipped required page review has `artifactStatus: generated` and `acceptanceStatus: PARTIAL`.

## 6. Success criteria

A completed Word handoff should report the final non-empty `.docx` path returned by the complete serial create/edit/repair/render-refresh chain, `artifactStatus`, `acceptanceStatus`, confirmed and completed design-unit counts, fourteen-topic local coverage, discovery/candidate closure counts, base-view and focused-figure counts, required rendered count, baseline/skeleton/late/replaced/removed/final relationship sets, visually reviewed count, missing count, bounded repairs, paragraph/table truncation status, and final render QA status. Any confirmed submodule without a complete local chapter, applicable content topic left `MISSING`, required figure left `MISSING`, incomplete visible-ID audit, or skipped required page review makes full acceptance `PARTIAL`, even when `artifactStatus: generated`. While native tools remain available and bounded attempts have not failed, continue from the first unresolved content unit or figure. At an exhausted execution boundary, persist state and do not claim or generate a complete final Word merely to end the turn. A Markdown draft, Mermaid source, ASCII diagram, temporary JSON file, or `08-word-export-input.md` alone is not a Word deliverable unless the user explicitly asked only for a draft.
